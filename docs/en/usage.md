# Step by step

## Create the plan

1. Put the widget **Project Plan** on the page, preferably in a
   wide column: A timeline needs space in the width. In a
   narrow column, it remains operable, but shows less at a time. 
2. Open the widget's settings. The plan editor opens from
   itself, filling the screen; it also covers the bar of the studio. 
3. An empty plan offers two ways: **Start empty** lays a level
   "Level 1"; if you prefer to start from a finished template, follow the
   Get started with a template section. 
4. In the header bar of the Plan Editor, under **Heading** 
   optionally enter a title for the plan. It stands above the plan and gives the
   Excel file their name. 
5. Create the layers in the **Layers** tab — see "Maintain layers". 
6. In the **Categories** tab, create the categories with their colors — 
   see "Maintaining categories". 
7. In the **Entries** tab, set the milestones, time periods, and deadlines
   — see the following sections. 
8. Click on **Apply** in the top right. 
9. In the dialog, check the buttons **Show Today Line** and
   **Offer Excel export**; both are pre-enabled. 
10. Save the page and preview the result. 

## Start with a template

1. Open the settings of a widget whose plan is still empty. 
2. In the Plan Editor, click **Start with Template**. The editor displays
   the templates as cards, each with thumbnail, description and what
   She brings: 
   - **Product roadmap** — several years and product lines based on the model
     of the Sales Truck Launch plan: three tiers, seven categories, and each
     Type of entry. 
   - **Postponement** — a postponed date over a few months: 
     old and new dates as key dates, one level per affected function. 
3. Click on the appropriate card. The editor will fill up with the template; 
   an already entered heading remains. **Back** leads without change
   back to the two paths. 
4. Customize the headline, layers, categories, and entries to fit your project
   or delete what you don't need. 
5. Click on **Apply** in the top right corner and save the page.

## Maintain layers

1. In the Plan Editor, open the **Layers** tab. 
2. To create a layer, click on **New Layer** and enter
   enter their name. You can also do it directly in the form of an entry: under
   **Classification** in the **Level** field, select the **New level ...**, select the
   Enter the name and click **Create** — the new layer is then the same
   assigned to the entry. 
3. To rename a layer, change its name directly in the list. 
   Next to each name is how many entries the layer contains. Each name
   may only occur once (regardless of upper and lower case). 
4. To change the order, move the layer with the arrows
   up or down. The order here is the order on the
   page and in the Excel export. 
5. To delete a layer, click **Delete**. Does it still contain
   entries, the editor asks if they are moved to another layer
   — which you select under **Target Level** — or whether they are also deleted
   . Confirm with **Delete** or break with **Cancel**

## Maintain categories

1. In the plan editor, open the **Categories** tab. 
2. To create a category, click on **New category** and
   enter their name — or select from the form of an entry
   under **Classification** in the **Category** field, select **New Category ...**; 
   there you enter the name, color and shape. 
3. Click on the color field in front of the name. It unfolds the twelve colors; 
   choose one of them or enter your own color under **Hex Value**
   for example '#E40045'. Esc or a click next to it closes the
   Selection. 
4. Click on the shape button next to it and select the shape with which the
   Milestones in this category appear: diamond, triangle, triangle with the tip down, square, circle, hexagon, star or cross. The arrow keys
   change from shape to shape. A new category gets the first form, which
   no one else is yet carrying. 
5. To change the order of the legend, move the category
   with the arrows pointing up or down. 
6. To delete a category, click **Delete**. Your entries
   are retained and become "without category" (grey); the query mentions, 
   how many entries this affects. 

## Create a milestone

1. In the plan editor, open the **Entries** tab. 
2. Select **Milestones** above the list and click **+**. The
   new entry is called "New milestone", is in the middle of the preview
   and in the first level; on the right, his form appears in the tab
   **General**, the title is highlighted. 
3. Enter the **title** and optionally a **description**. The
   Description appears in the details, line breaks are retained. 
4. Under **Classification**, select **Level** and **Category** — 
   or "Without Category". 
5. Enter the **date** under **Date**. 
6. Shape and color of the milestone come from its category; without
   category it appears as a gray diamond. Under **Classification** there is a
   Note with the shape it is currently wearing. 
7. Set **Tentative** if the date is not yet fixed. 
8. Click on **Apply** in the top right corner once all entries are finished
   are.

## Combine entries into a series

1. In the **Entries** tab, select the first entry in the series and
   open **Classification**. 
2. Click in the **Series** field. It unfolds the series that it has in
   of this layer, each with the number of its entries. Tap
   a new name, for example "TG Assist MY26", and select
   **Create "TG Assist MY26" as a new series** (or press Enter). 
3. Select the next entry of the same level and select
   **Series** the series you just created from the list. 
4. Repeat step 3 for all entries in the series. **No series** all the way
   at the top of the list takes out an entry; Esc represents the
   previous value. 

Series are only valid within one level. Upper and lower case plays
no role when found again, hyphens and accents do: "TG Assist
MY26" and "TG-Assist MY26" are two different series. 

## Set a dependency

1. In the **Entries** tab, select the entry that has been created by another
   (the successor), and open **Dependencies**. 
2. Under **Depends on**, find and select the predecessor. Multiple
   Predecessors are possible. 
3. On the page, a dotted line with an arrow now connects the
   Predecessor with successor. 

Dependencies only exist between milestones and time periods, not for
deadlines. If an entry is deleted, it will also disappear from all
Dependencies.

## Change afterwards

1. Open the widget's settings again. The Plan Editor will display the
   saved plan. 
2. Select the entry in the list of the **Entries** tab or by
   one click in the preview and modify its form. One click in
   of the preview switches to the **Entries** tab and fetches the
   Entry in the list in sight. 
3. Click on **Apply** in the top right corner and save the page. 
   The date after "Status:" is set to the day of the change. 

As long as changes have not yet been applied, the header bar says
"Unsaved changes". Click **Cancel** to discard all changes
since the last time the plan editor was opened; the editor asks beforehand. 

## Download the plan as an Excel spreadsheet (as a reader)

1. Filter and zoom the plan so that you can see what you need. 
2. Click **Export** in the toolbar. On narrow
   screens, the button is in the menu **More**. 
3. Choose the scope: 
   - **Current View** — only the entries in the visible section that
     meet all active filters; if the search is active, only the hits. 
   - **Full plan** — all entries, without filters and sections. 

   Behind each election is how many entries it contains. 
4. Click on **Download Excel**. The file is named like the
   Heading of the plan with today's date, without heading
   'Projektplan_JJJJ-MM-TT.xlsx'. 

The file contains the sheet **Planning** with one line per entry (level, 
Type, Title, Category, Start, End, Series, Preliminary, Predecessor, 
description) and the sheet **Info** with title, status, export date, scope and
the active filters.
