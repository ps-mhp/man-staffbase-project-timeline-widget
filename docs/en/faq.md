# FAQ

**Question:** The page says "No entries for these filters". 

Answer: The selected filters do not leave any entry — for example, because all
categories are hidden in the legend, a level is deselected, or the
Period filter does not touch any entry. Click **Filter
reset**; then the whole plan can be seen again. Filters only apply to
Her own visit, other readers see the plan unfiltered. 

**Question:** When exporting, "The export failed. Please re-export
." 

Answer: The Excel file could not be generated. The tool for this is
only loaded when clicking on **Export**; often the connection was
briefly interrupted at that moment. Click on **Excel again.
Download**. If it doesn't work repeatedly, reload the page. 

**Question:** At the top of the plan editor it says "N entries could not be read
and are lost when saving." 

Answer: In the saved plan there are entries with which the widget does not
— for example, without a title, with an invalid date, or in a layer that
no longer exists. Such entries are already shown on the published page
not. Save the plan, they disappear permanently. Do you want to
, click **Cancel** instead of apply, and apply
to the person who last changed the plan — usually the content was
of the **Plan** text box by hand. 

**Question:** When turning the mouse wheel, "Ctrl/⌘ pressed to zoom" appears
". 

Answer: The mouse wheel alone scrolls the page, just like everywhere else — otherwise
you would get stuck on the map while scrolling. To zoom, hold down the Ctrl key
(Mac: ⌘) and turn the mouse wheel. The message only appears
once. 

**Question:** The plan editor says "No entries yet — the preview will appear
with the first entry." 

Answer: The plan does not yet have an entry, so there is nothing to show. 
In the **Entries** tab, under **Add**, set a milestone, 
Select the time period or deadline, or click **Start with Sample Plan**. 

**Question:** How do I zoom? 

Answer: In four ways: with the **−** and **+** buttons in the
toolbar; with the Ctrl key pressed (Mac: ⌘) and the mouse wheel, then
the plan zooms around the spot under the mouse pointer; with two fingers on
trackpad or touchscreen; or with the keyboard via **+** and **−**, as soon as
the plan has the focus. **Show all** (key **0**) shows the whole
Plan. The section can be moved by dragging with the mouse,
Shift and the mouse wheel or via the frame in the overview below the sheet. 

**Question:** Why don't I see the "Export" button? 

Answer: Either **Excel Export is set in the settings of the widget
or the screen is narrow (less than 768 pixels)
width): then **Export** is in the **More** menu of the toolbar. 

**Question:** What does a dashed border or a symbol that is only used as a
outline? 

Answer: The entry is marked as **preliminary** — its date is still pending
not fixed, in the template it said "tbd". Milestones then only appear
as an outline, periods filled brighter with dashed border. In the details
It also says "provisional". 

**Question:** What do the dotted lines mean? 

Answer: There are two types. An **oblique or horizontal dashed
Line with arrow** is a dependency: it leads from the predecessor to the
Successor. A **vertical dashed line** through all layers, at the bottom
with a triangle and a title, is a deadline that applies to the whole plan
applies. 

**Question:** A dependency has disappeared. 

Answer: The line only appears if both ends are visible. If the
Predecessor or successor hidden by a filter or its layer
deselected, the line is missing. If one of the two entries has been deleted, the
Dependency removed for good. 

**Question:** The entries of a series are not on one line. 

Answer: A series is valid only within one level, and the name must be exactly
— "TG Assist MY26" and "TG-Assist MY26" are two series. 
In the form of each entry, check the **Series** field and its **level**.

**Question:** The plan editor says "A plan has more than 300 entries
not." (or the same for 20 levels or 24 categories). 

Answer: A plan has a maximum of 300 entries, 20 levels, and
24 categories; nothing more can be added at the border. The editor
shows at the top right how many entries the plan already has. For very large
If you plan to do this, it is recommended to split the plan into several widgets, such as
one per series.

**Question:** The form says "Please enter a title.", "Please enter a title."
Enter your name.", "Please enter a date.", "Please enter a valid date
", "The end is before the beginning.", "The beginning is after the
End." or "Please specify a color as #RRGGBB, such as #E40045." 

Answer: The input in the field cannot be used in this way. As long as the message
, the plan retains the last valid value — so nothing works
lost, but the change hasn't arrived yet either. Correct
the input; as soon as it is valid, the message disappears. In the case of a
Period, first change the date that restores the order: 
Should it start and end later, first **end**, then **beginning**. 

**Question:** What happens to the plan during the automatic translation of the
Side? 

Answer: The title of the plan, the titles of the levels and the
categories as well as the title and description of each entry. Dates, colors, 
Dependencies and the series names remain unchanged — a series name is
one key, and two differently translated versions would form a series
tear apart.

could not be inserted. It remains in the source language and must be
manually translated."
