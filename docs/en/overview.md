# Project plan

This widget shows a project plan as a **timeline** — such as the roadmap
a vehicle launch with trade fairs, series start-ups (SOPs) and
project milestones over several years. It replaces the PowerPoint slide, 
which has so far been maintained for such plans and integrated as an image: The Plan
is directly on the page, can be changed at any time, and readers
can explore it yourself, filter it and download it as an Excel spreadsheet. 

The entire plan is maintained in the **Plan Editor**, which is displayed when the
settings by itself. 

## What a plan consists of

- **Heading** — optional; stands above the plan and gives the Excel file
  their name. 
- **Levels** — horizontal paths among each other, for example "Measuring", 
  "Launches / SOPs" and "Project Milestones". On the left is their title. 
- **Categories** — give the entries their color and appear as a legend
  above the plan, for example "MY26 TG Assist" in purple. 
- **Entries** in three types: 
  - **Milestone** — a single date, shown as a symbol (rhombus, 
    triangle, square or circle) with the title below. 
  - **Period** — a bar from start to finish, with a
    Arrowhead at the end for "keep running". 
  - **Deadline** — a date that applies to all levels, such as a new one
    regulation. It appears as a dashed vertical line through the whole
    Plan, with the title below. 
- **Series** — Milestones and periods of the same level with the same
  Series names are on a common line. If it includes a time period, 
  the milestones sit on its beam; otherwise a narrow
  Do the first with the last. 
- **Dependencies** — dashed lines with arrow from the predecessor to the
  successor, for example from "C4S" to the associated SOP. 
- **Preliminary** — an entry whose date has not yet been determined. It
  appears only as an outline or brightly filled with a dashed border. 

## What readers see

From top to bottom: 

1. **Heading** (if set) and **"Status: ..."** — the date of the last
   Change to the plan, in the date format of the page language. 
2. **Toolbar** — Search, **Filter**, Zoom (**−**, **+**, **All
   show**), the toggle **Timeline | List**, **Export**, 
   **Full screen** and **Help**. 
3. **Legend** — categories with their color. One click displays a
   Category off or on again. 
4. **The plan** — on the left the titles of the layers, on the right the timeline with the
   entries, below the last level the titles of the key dates. The arrow on the
   Title collapses one layer; **Collapse all** above the titles collapses
   all at once and then open again. 
5. **Overview** — a narrow strip over the entire period. A frame
   shows which section is currently being seen. 

Also: 

- **Zoom** is infinitely variable: via the **−** and **+** buttons, with the
  Ctrl key (Mac: ⌘) and the mouse wheel or with two fingers on the trackpad and
  touchscreen. The axis changes from years to quarters and months to
  to calendar weeks and individual days. 
- **Move** is done by dragging with the mouse, with Shift and the mouse wheel, 
  by wiping horizontally or by dragging the frame in the overview. 
- A **click on an entry** opens its details: type and date, 
  Category, level, series, description, as well as predecessors and successors. 
- **Filters** and **Search** only apply to your own visit; saved or
  nothing is passed on to others. 
- **List** shows the same entries as a table, sorted by date — the
  more convenient way for screen readers, narrow screens and for printing. 
- **Export** downloads the entries as an Excel file, provided that the
  Export is turned on in Settings. 
- A dark line **"Today"** marks today, if it
  is turned on and the tag is in the visible section. 
- The operation also works without a mouse: Tab jumps into the plan, the
  Arrow keys switch between entries, Enter opens the details, 
  **+** and **−** zoom, **0** shows everything. 
- On narrow screens (less than 768 pixels wide), filters and
  Details as a sheet from the bottom, and **List** and **Export** are available
  in the menu **More**. 
- **Full screen** shows the plan across the entire screen, with the same
  operation; only the plan itself rolls, axis and overview remain standing. 
  **Esc** or **Exit Full Screen** returns to the page. Where the browser does not
  real full screen (such as Safari on the iPhone), the plan overlays
  the whole page. 
- **Help** explains to readers directly on the page what the characters
  (Legend tab, with the categories of this plan) and how to use the
  Plan operated with mouse, trackpad, touch and keyboard (**Operation** tab). 
  From the plan, the **?** key also opens the help. In the plan editor,
  it doesn't have the button. 
- **Without entries, the widget doesn't show anything** — no empty frame and
  no error message. 

## What you see in the CMS editor

The plan editor includes a **preview** at the top: the same timeline as on
of the page, with zoom, but without filters, list and export. Click on a
Entry in the preview selects it for editing. How readers use the plan
with all filters and the export, check in the preview of the page.
